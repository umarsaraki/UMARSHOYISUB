Privacy Policy

Last updated:

UMARSHOYI-SUB ("we", "us") provides a platform for purchasing airtime, data, and related digital services. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Full name, username, email address, phone number, and transaction history. Your transaction PIN is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot see or recover it. We never store your card or bank details; those are handled directly by our licensed payment processor.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, process your purchases with our airtime/data supplier, process payments with our payment processor, and communicate with you about your transactions.

Sharing

We share only what's necessary with our airtime/data provider (to fulfil your order) and our payment processor (to process funding). We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data or request account deletion at any time by contacting support.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@yourdomain.com

Terms & Conditions

Last updated:

1. Service

UMARSHOYI-SUB lets you fund a wallet and use the balance to purchase airtime, data, and other digital services from third-party providers. We are an intermediary, not the network operator.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate information when registering and keep your password and transaction PIN confidential. You are responsible for activity on your account.

3. Wallet & Payments

Wallet funding is processed by a licensed third-party payment processor. Funds are non-refundable except where a purchase fails to deliver, in which case the amount is reversed to your wallet.

4. Purchases

Double-check the recipient number before confirming any purchase — delivered airtime/data cannot be reversed once sent successfully.

5. Prohibited use

You may not use UMARSHOYI-SUB for fraudulent transactions, money laundering, or any unlawful purpose.

6. Limitation of liability

We are not liable for delays or failures caused by our upstream network or payment providers, beyond reversing the affected transaction.
